IMPORTANT POSITIONING

Do NOT position Snow Pond as a generic managed IT provider.

Do NOT focus primarily on products, software, cybersecurity tools, Microsoft 365, or technical features.

Instead focus on business outcomes.

The website should speak directly to:

- HVAC contractors
- Electrical contractors
- Landscaping contractors
- Manufacturing companies

Secondary audience:

- Engineering firms
- General contractors

Messaging should focus on:

- Reducing business downtime
- Improving employee productivity
- Supporting growth
- Protecting the company from cyber threats
- Keeping office and field employees connected
- Improving communication and collaboration
- Meeting cyber insurance requirements
- Reducing technology frustration
- Helping owners focus on running their business

The site should feel more like a business advisor and strategic partner than a technology company.
